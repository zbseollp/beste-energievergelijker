## How do you find the best energy provider in the Netherlands?

If you are living in the Netherlands, finding a new energy provider can be a daunting task. The Dutch energy market is completely liberalised, which means that over 25 different suppliers are licensed to offer gas and electricity. As a consumer, you are completely free to select the provider that best meets your requirements. However, because there are so many alternatives available, it might be difficult to know where to begin.

One way to simplify the process is to use a energy comparison website. These websites allow you to compare the prices and services offered by different providers in order to find the one that is right for you. They are a great way to save time and money, and can help you make an informed decision about which supplier is right for you.

## Electricity price Netherlands

The price of electricity in the Netherlands is relatively low compared to other European countries. In 2020, the average price per kilowatt hour for households using between 1.000 and 2.500 kWh was 5.45 euro cents. If your annual consumption is between 1.000 and 2.500 kWh, you’ll pay less than consumers using between 2.500 and 5.000 kWh annually.

If you want to change energy provider in the Netherlands, be sure to check out our comparison tool to find the best provider that suit your needs.

## Gas price Netherlands

Are you looking for a new energy provider in the Netherland? If so, you’ll be interested in the latest news on gas prices in the Netherlands. A year ago, a typical four-person household paid around 500 euros for the energy bills (gas and electricity), excluding taxes and fees. Now that price lays around 1.200 euros for the same consumption, more than twice as much as before.

This dramatic price increase can be explained by rising gas prices. The price increase in gas, in turn, can be explained by a shortage of natural gas. Experts say this is due in part to the long, cold winter of 2022 and maintenance on the gas grid in Europe.

Before you start comparing, it is wise to know what your energy consumption is. Based on this information, we can specifically look for the best supplier for your situation. So fill in what your electricity and gas consumption is and whether you have a single or double meter.

In addition, you can also indicate whether you have solar panels so that you can find the cheapest contract for energy in combination with solar panels.

If you do not know what your consumption is? Then you can click on the ‘icons’ to see what the estimated consumption is for a household with a certain number of people.

### Step 2: Address information

For the next step, we need some information about your address. Simply enter your zip code and house number, and any additions to the house number, in the same box.

### Step 3: Compare the different energy suppliers

Now that you have entered some basic information you can press the dark blue button on the right with ‘Vergelijken’ (Compare) on it. Based on your information, the system will then look at which energy supplier is the cheapest for your situation. Compare the top suppliers with each other to find the best one for you.

Want to search even more specifically for certain needs? Then use the handy filters. These allow you to indicate what type of contract you are looking for. Choose for example the desired duration of the contract: variable, 1 year, 2 years, 3 years or 5 years.

### Step 5: Choose the best one for your situation

Once you have filled in all your preferences you will find a selection of energy providers that meet your needs. Compare them to make sure you choose the right one.
